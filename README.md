# STEMUchkun-
Free STEM Education Resources | Kyrgyzstan

STEM Uchkun is a student-led initiative dedicated to making STEM education more accessible, particularly for girls in remote regions of Kyrgyzstan.

Our Mission

To improve access to quality educational materials by translating and adapting mathematics, biology, and chemistry resources into Kyrgyz and Russian for students in Grades 7–10.

What We Do

- Translate and adapt STEM educational resources.
- Share free learning materials through Telegram.
- Promote inclusive STEM education in underserved communities.

Subjects

- Mathematics
- Biology
- Chemistry
